# Business Insights – Supermarket Sales Analysis

## Key Findings

1. Total Sales generated: 322.97K
2. Total Profit: 15.38K
3. Total Quantity Sold: 5,510

## Product Performance
- Food & Beverages is the top-performing category.
- Health & Beauty has the lowest sales.
- Focus on promoting low-performing categories.

## City Performance
- Naypyitaw generated the highest sales.
- Mandalay and Yangon show balanced performance.
- Marketing strategies can be optimized city-wise.

## Sales Trend
- Sales show a slight downward trend over time.
- Seasonal or operational factors may be affecting performance.

## Recommendations
- Increase promotions for low-performing product lines.
- Focus inventory planning on high-demand categories.
- Implement targeted marketing campaigns for underperforming cities.
- Monitor sales trends monthly for better forecasting.
